Why it scored this way

0
1. Exact amount for every active ingredient

Four probiotic blends disclose blend totals (224/36/36/13 mg) but no per-strain amounts.

1
2. Standardization percentage stated

MAPP pomegranate extract stated as >40% polyphenolic + phenolic bioactives — an earned point, not the default.

1
3. Chemical form or strain named

All 24 organisms carry strain designations (B. longum SD-BB536-JP, L. rhamnosus HRVD113-US, etc.); first product in the pilot to take this point under v1.6.

0
4. Dose within the trial range

Under methodology v1.7. Previously 1, on the DS-01 finished-product trials. Those no longer set the range: they dose a whole multi-ingredient synbiotic, so the GI benefit cannot be attributed to the pomegranate component, and their authors include manufacturer employees while an independent ingredient-level range exists. Scored instead against that independent range — 320-656 mg phenolics — which the label's 400 mg at >40% meets only to a floor of about 160 mg, at or below the dose an independent dose-response trial found null (PMID 27879044). Probiotics excluded per methodology.

0
5. Certificate of analysis is public

No publicly accessible third-party certificate of analysis located 2026-09-07 (product page, brand site, site-scoped search, retail listing images); recorded as a negative finding, not a claim that none exists.

0
6. Per-serving amounts are complete

Serving size 2 ViaCaps stated but servings per container is absent from the panel.
