Why it scored this way

60ct variant, Amazon listing B08Y66H6B7.

1
1. Exact amount for every active ingredient

Both actives carry exact amounts, Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG 46 mg (12 billion CFUs) and Inulin (Chicory Root Extract) 200 mg. No proprietary blend on the panel. First product in the set to take this point.

1
2. Standardization percentage stated

No standardization-dependent ingredient. The probiotic is CFU-declared and inulin is named as the compound with its source in parentheses, so the quantified item is the inulin, not an extract of graded purity. This is the distinction from BIOMA, where the label declared 100 mg of corn cob extract rather than a stated XOS weight.

1
3. Chemical form or strain named

Strain designation present (rhamnosus GG), and inulin is a defined compound with its source named.

0
4. Dose within the trial range

Inulin 200 mg against an independent reference range of 12 g/d for functional constipation (Puhlmann 2025, PMID 41233756), roughly sixty times below the studied dose. Probiotics excluded per methodology.

0
5. Certificate of analysis is public

No publicly accessible third-party certificate of analysis located 2026-09-26 (product page images, culturelle.com, site-scoped search, open web search); recorded as a negative finding, not a claim that none exists.

0
6. Per-serving amounts are complete

Serving size 'One (1) Capsule' is stated but servings per container is absent from the Supplement Facts panel, the same failure as Seed DS-01.
