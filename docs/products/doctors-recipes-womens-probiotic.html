Why it scored this way

60ct variant, Amazon listing B07YWJZJNY. Included under the v1.9 ruling, since digestive is the first benefit named and the product is not vaginal-specific.

0
1. Exact amount for every active ingredient

Two proprietary blends. Probiotic Blend gives only an aggregate 50 Billion CFUs across 16 organisms, and Prebiotic Fiber Blend gives only a 250 mg total with no composition listed at all, not even ingredient names. Organic Cranberry Extract 50 mg is the single line carrying an exact amount.

0
2. Standardization percentage stated

Organic Cranberry Extract is declared as 50 mg of extract with no proanthocyanidin percentage. Cranberry is the textbook standardized botanical, its urinary-tract literature doses in mg of PACs, and 50 mg of unspecified extract cannot be converted into that unit. This is a cleaner instance of the same functional test applied to BIOMA's XOS. The Prebiotic Fiber Blend compounds it by naming no ingredients whatsoever.

0
3. Chemical form or strain named

All 16 organisms are named to genus and species only (Lactobacillus rhamnosus, Bifidobacterium longum and so on) with no strain designations. The listing title and the bottle front both advertise '16 Strains' while the panel identifies none of them, the same gap as rank 16.

0
4. Dose within the trial range

The probiotics are CFU-declared with no strain designations and only an aggregate count, so no organism can be matched to a trial. Under v1.13 there is no through-expiry guarantee anywhere on the panel, and no at-manufacture qualifier either, so the delivered dose cannot be established. The Prebiotic Fiber Blend has no disclosed composition. Cranberry at 50 mg is unstandardized, and its trial literature addresses urinary tract outcomes rather than the digestive outcome this product is scored against.

0
5. Certificate of analysis is public

No publicly accessible third-party certificate of analysis located 2026-09-27 (listing images, Amazon brand store, site-scoped search). Listing image 6 presents a 'Quality Checked at Every Stage' graphic with Testing and Additional Testing steps, which is a claim with no document behind it and is explicitly not a certificate under the methodology. Recorded as a negative finding, not a claim that none exists.
