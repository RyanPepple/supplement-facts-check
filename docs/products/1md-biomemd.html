1. Exact amount for every active ingredient

Two blends. The BiomeMD Probiotic Blend gives a 121 mg total for 13 organisms with no per-organism amount, and the Advanced Prebiotic Blend gives a 380 mg total for three fibers with no per-fiber amount. UABla-12 and DDS-1 are the only actives with their own amounts.

0
2. Standardization percentage stated

XOS is sold at graded purities and its reference range is expressed as XOS, so a percentage is potency-determining under the functional test; here neither the percentage nor even an XOS weight is disclosed, since it sits inside a blend total. Scored consistently with BIOMA, Lemme Debloat and Garden of Life Men.

0
3. Chemical form or strain named

The 13 organisms in the BiomeMD Probiotic Blend are named to genus and species only (Lactobacillus plantarum, Bacillus coagulans and so on). UABla-12 and DDS-1 carry strain designations, but one undesignated organism is enough to score 0 under v1.6.

0
4. Dose within the trial range

On the fibers alone. The Advanced Prebiotic Blend is 380 mg in total, so even if all of it were XOS it would sit below the 1.4 g/day low end of the XOS range, and even if all of it were FOS it would be under a tenth of the 5 g/day FOS range: the same upper-bound test applied to Physician's CHOICE's 150 mg prebiotic blend. The probiotics give no point back. The 13 blended organisms cannot be compared to any trial dose under the attribution rule. UABla-12 and DDS-1 are declared both in milligrams and in CFU. The milligram figures cannot be used under v1.12: the human trial located for these strains at a digestive outcome, Martoni 2020 (PMID 32019158, randomised double-blind placebo-controlled, 330 adults with IBS, conducted with the strain supplier UAS Laboratories, so the weaker basis under the independence rule), doses in CFU, not milligrams of preparation: 1 x 10^10 CFU/day per strain (Martoni 2020 full text, mdpi.com), the same figure as the label's unqualified 10 billion CFU, so the 0 rests on the v1.13 rule that an unqualified count does not establish a delivered dose. Its introduction cites earlier trials of the same strains in functional constipation and lactose intolerance; those were not reviewed for this row, which is recorded as a limit of the search (PubMed 'Lactobacillus acidophilus DDS-1 irritable bowel randomized', 2026-11-19, 2 records). The CFU figures carry no through-expiry guarantee anywhere on the panel, so under v1.13 the delivered dose cannot be established and criterion 4 scores 0 on both ingredients.
