Why it scored this way

28ct variant, Amazon listing B0BS5L5JBH.

0
1. Exact amount for every active ingredient

DIGEZYME Multi-enzyme Complex 23 mg is a blend total covering five enzymes (alpha-amylase, neutral protease, lactase, cellulase, lipase) with no per-enzyme amounts. Vitamin B12 2.4 mcg and the probiotic 10 mg do carry exact amounts, but a proprietary blend anywhere on the panel fails the criterion.

0
2. Standardization percentage stated

Enzyme potency is expressed in activity units (alpha-amylase in DU, protease in HUT, lactase in ALU, cellulase in CU, lipase in FIP), not in milligrams. A combined weight with no activity declaration cannot be converted into the unit the evidence uses, which is the functional test stated in the methodology. Same judgment as the BIOMA XOS row; flagged on the same basis.

1
3. Chemical form or strain named

Every form is named. Vitamin B12 as cyanocobalamin, the probiotic carries strain designation 35624, and the five enzymes are each named individually rather than as an undifferentiated 'enzyme blend'.

0
4. Dose within the trial range

Under methodology v1.13. The probiotic declares 1x10^9 CFU at manufacture but guarantees only 1x10^7 through the best-by date, ten times below the 1x10^8 capsule dose Whorwell 2006 (PMID 16863564) found superior to placebo. Vitamin B12 at 2.4 mcg is exactly the Daily Value, but the claimed outcome is bloating relief and food digestion rather than B12 repletion, and no adequate human trial was identified for B12 at that outcome. The enzymes sit inside a blend total, so none can be compared to a reference range under the attribution rule.

0
5. Certificate of analysis is public

No publicly accessible third-party certificate of analysis located 2026-09-27. Same search as the Align Pro Formula row and the same limitation: the alignprobiotics.com footer could not be walked directly because the domain is blocked to this session's browser. Recorded as a negative finding, not a claim that none exists.

1
6. Per-serving amounts are complete

Serving size 1 capsule and servings per container 28 are both printed on the panel. Note: the DIGEZYME weight is 23 mg, not 20 mg. The lower figure is what the listing thumbnail appears to show and was corrected by reading the panel at native resolution.
