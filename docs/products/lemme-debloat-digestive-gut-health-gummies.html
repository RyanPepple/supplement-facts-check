Why it scored this way

60ct variant, Amazon listing B0BHJ55LNQ.

0
1. Exact amount for every active ingredient

'Digestive Probiotic Blend 44 mg' gives a blend total for two organisms with no per-organism amount, and notably no CFU count anywhere on the panel at all, which is unusual even among the blends in this sample. Xylooligosaccharides 80 mg does carry an exact amount.

0
2. Standardization percentage stated

XOS is declared as 80 mg with no purity percentage. This is the same case as BIOMA, where commercial XOS is sold at graded purities from roughly 35 to 95 percent and the reference range is expressed as XOS rather than as extract weight. Scored consistently with that row.

1
3. Chemical form or strain named

Both organisms carry strain designations, Bacillus coagulans MTCC 5856 (LactoSpore) and Bacillus subtilis DE111. The listing's claim of 'two clinically studied strains' is, unlike several other products in this sample, actually supported by the panel.

0
4. Dose within the trial range

XOS at 80 mg falls far below the 1.4 to 8 g/day reference range, reaching under 6 percent of the low end even if the 80 mg were 100 percent XOS, which the label does not establish. The probiotics sit inside a 44 mg blend total with no CFU declared, so neither strain can be compared to any trial dose under the attribution rule, and under v1.13 no delivered potency is established either.

0
5. Certificate of analysis is public

No publicly accessible third-party certificate of analysis located 2026-09-27 (listing images, brand site, site-scoped and open web search). Listing image 5 displays a Clean Label Project Purity Award, which is a certification mark rather than a product certificate of analysis and is excluded on the same basis the methodology excludes GMP and facility certificates. Recorded as a negative finding, not a claim that none exists.

1
6. Per-serving amounts are complete

Serving size 2 gummies and servings per container 30 are both printed on the panel.
