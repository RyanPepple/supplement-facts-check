2. Standardization percentage stated

By default. Nothing in the formula is standardization-dependent: the probiotics are declared by CFU and weight, and FOS is declared as the compound itself (fructo-oligosaccharides) rather than as an extract weight, the same distinction applied to Culturelle Pro Strength's inulin and not available to BIOMA's XOS.

0
3. Chemical form or strain named

Ten of the eleven organisms are named to species only; only Bacillus coagulans MTCC 5856 carries a strain designation, and one undesignated organism is enough to score 0 under v1.6.

0
4. Dose within the trial range

The probiotics cannot be compared to any trial dose, since every organism sits inside a blend total (attribution rule) and the panel gives no through-expiry guarantee (the brand's own product page FAQ states guaranteed potency through the expiration date, but that is not on the label read here). That leaves FOS, declared at 50 mg against a reference range of 5 g/day (clinical-doses.csv, Paineau 2008, PMID 17697398), so the disclosed dose is one hundredth of the only positive-outcome trial dose and falls outside it.

Other ingredients: Delayed Release Vegetable Cellulose Capsule, Rice Flour, Silica.

Does the 1MD Complete Probiotics Platinum label give an exact amount for every ingredient?

No. At least one active ingredient is listed without its own amount, for example inside a blend total, so it scored 0 on criterion 1. The 11 organisms sit inside a single 308 mg blend total with no per-organism amount or CFU split. NutraFlora FOS at 50 mg does carry an exact amount.

Is 1MD Complete Probiotics Platinum third-party tested?

This audit checks one thing here: whether a third-party certificate of analysis (COA) for the product can be reached by the public, with no email gate and no account. None was located, so it scored 0 on criterion 5. That records what a documented search found, not a claim that the product was never tested.
