4. Dose within the trial range

Under methodology v1.12, the first product in the sample to take this point. The panel declares 500 mg of preparation rather than CFU, so the probiotic unit mismatch does not arise, and 500 mg falls at the floor of the 500-600 mg/day range set by adult trials of this strain (Seddik 2019 PMID 30694338, open-label; Salazar-Parra 2023 PMID 37400812). Corrected 2026-11-19: Sjomina 2023 (PMID 37942999) was previously counted and set the 1000 mg upper bound, but its own conclusion is that S. boulardii did not increase eradication and its positive subgroup results lost significance after Type I error adjustment, so under the positive-outcome rule it does not set the range. The score is unchanged. Maev 2025 excluded from setting the range under the independence rule: it dosed Enterol, a Biocodex product, and Biocodex manufactures Florastor. The outcome objection is published alongside the v1.12 ruling.
