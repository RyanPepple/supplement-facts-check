3. Chemical form or strain named

CNCM I-745 is a culture depositary designation (Collection Nationale de Cultures de Microorganismes), not a species name or a marketing name.

1
4. Dose within the trial range

Under methodology v1.12, the first product in the sample to take this point. The panel declares 500 mg of preparation rather than CFU, so the probiotic unit mismatch does not arise, and 500 mg falls at the floor of the 500-1000 mg/day range set by independent adult trials of this strain (Seddik 2019 PMID 30694338; Sjomina 2023 PMID 37942999; Salazar-Parra 2023 PMID 37400812). Maev 2025 excluded from setting the range under the independence rule: it dosed Enterol, a Biocodex product, and Biocodex manufactures Florastor. The outcome objection is published alongside the v1.12 ruling.
