Fiber supplements: label disclosure scores

23 fiber formulas from the top 30 of Amazon Best Sellers in Fiber Supplements, captured 11/19/2026, scored on the same six criteria as the probiotic sample. This is a separate sample, so none of these scores is counted in any figure on the probiotic results.

What the labels show

!
3 of 23 state a daily dose that lands in a trial-backed range.

OLLY Fiber Gummy Rings, Konsyl Daily Psyllium Fiber Powder, GOBIOTIX Prebiotic Fiber Boost. Most gummies deliver 4 to 8 g of fiber a day, where the inulin and polydextrose trials that found an effect used 8 to 12 g.

!
0 of 23 publish a certificate of analysis.

Several carry NSF, USP or Informed Choice certification, which the product pages note. A certification listing is not a certificate of analysis a buyer can match to a lot.

✓
18 of 23 disclose an exact amount for every active.

Most fiber products have one fiber, and the fiber line states it. The ones that fail mix two or more fibers under one total.

Highest score: 5/6. Score spread: 3 at 5/6, 12 at 4/6, 6 at 3/6, 2 at 2/6. Read the fiber rulings in the methodology for how fiber labels are read, including the objection that fiber grams understate psyllium husk weight, which decides two close calls flagged on their pages.

How the sample was built

The top 30 listings were captured on 11/19/2026 and each was kept only if its first-listed claim is regularity or digestive health. Each distinct formula is scored once, at its highest rank; a repeat size whose panel matches ingredient for ingredient and amount for amount is recorded as a duplicate. Every decision is in fiber-sample.csv.

Excluded

  • Rank 8, NOW Supplements Psyllium Husk Powder: First-listed claim is heart-disease risk reduction; the title names no benefit.
  • Rank 9, FOODOLOGY Coleology Cutting Jelly: First-listed claim is slimming / GLP-1 weight management.

Duplicates, not scored twice

  • Rank 12, Benefiber Prebiotic Fiber Supplement Powder, Unflavored, 28 Stick Packs: duplicate of rank 7
  • Rank 14, Metamucil On-The-Go Psyllium Husk Powder Packets, Sugar-Free, Orange: duplicate of rank 2
  • Rank 16, Benefiber Prebiotic Fiber Supplement Powder, Unflavored, 124 Teaspoons: duplicate of rank 7
  • Rank 21, Metamucil 3-in-1 Fiber Capsules, 340 Capsules: duplicate of rank 18

Not scored

  • Rank 22, Metamucil Fiber Thins, Apple Crisp: No Supplement Facts panel or ingredient list located (all Amazon listing images; metamucil.com Fiber Thins pages, 2026-11-19).
