Where they differ

The pair splits on 3 of the six criteria. The reasoning below is the same text that appears on each product page.

0 / 1
1. Exact amount for every active ingredient

Physician's CHOICE Probiotics 60 Billion CFU: Probiotic Blend 220 mg and Prebiotic Blend 150 mg give blend totals only, no per-ingredient amounts.

Culturelle Pro Strength Probiotics: Both actives carry exact amounts, Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG 46 mg (12 billion CFUs) and Inulin (Chicory Root Extract) 200 mg. No proprietary blend on the panel. First product in the set to take this point.

0 / 1
3. Chemical form or strain named

Physician's CHOICE Probiotics 60 Billion CFU: Ten organisms named to species only (Lacticaseibacillus casei, Lactobacillus acidophilus, ... Lactobacillus bulgaricus), no strain designations, per methodology v1.6.

Culturelle Pro Strength Probiotics: Strain designation present (rhamnosus GG), and inulin is a defined compound with its source named.

1 / 0
6. Per-serving amounts are complete

Physician's CHOICE Probiotics 60 Billion CFU: Serving size 1 capsule and 30 servings per container both stated.

Culturelle Pro Strength Probiotics: Serving size 'One (1) Capsule' is stated but servings per container is absent from the Supplement Facts panel, the same failure as Seed DS-01.

Where they agree

Both labels were scored the same way on the remaining 3.

1 / 1
2. Standardization percentage stated

Physician's CHOICE Probiotics 60 Billion CFU: No standardization-dependent ingredient in the formula.

Culturelle Pro Strength Probiotics: No standardization-dependent ingredient. The probiotic is CFU-declared and inulin is named as the compound with its source in parentheses, so the quantified item is the inulin, not an extract of graded purity. This is the distinction from BIOMA, where the label declared 100 mg of corn cob extract rather than a stated XOS weight.

0 / 0
4. Dose within the trial range

Physician's CHOICE Probiotics 60 Billion CFU: Prebiotic Blend totals 150 mg including inulin from Jerusalem artichoke and chicory, an order of magnitude below the 12 g/d inulin reference range even if the blend were entirely inulin; probiotics excluded per methodology.

Culturelle Pro Strength Probiotics: Inulin 200 mg against an independent reference range of 12 g/d for functional constipation (Puhlmann 2025, PMID 41233756), roughly sixty times below the studied dose. Probiotics excluded per methodology.

0 / 0
5. Certificate of analysis is public

Physician's CHOICE Probiotics 60 Billion CFU: No publicly accessible third-party certificate of analysis located 2026-09-07 (product page, brand site, site-scoped search, retail listing images); recorded as a negative finding, not a claim that none exists.

Culturelle Pro Strength Probiotics: No publicly accessible third-party certificate of analysis located 2026-09-26 (product page images, culturelle.com, site-scoped search, open web search); recorded as a negative finding, not a claim that none exists.
