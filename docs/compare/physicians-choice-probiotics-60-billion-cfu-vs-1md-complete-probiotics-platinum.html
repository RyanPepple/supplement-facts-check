Head to head

Physician's CHOICE Probiotics 60 Billion CFU vs 1MD Complete Probiotics Platinum

Both score 2 out of 6, and on the identical criteria. On label disclosure there is nothing to choose between them.

What this compares. Six yes-or-no questions about what each label discloses, scored under methodology v1.15 from dated screenshots of the two live labels (Physician's CHOICE Probiotics 60 Billion CFU captured 2026-09-07, 1MD Complete Probiotics Platinum captured 2026-11-19). A higher score means the label tells you more, not that the product works better. Neither product was lab-tested, and nothing here is medical advice or a recommendation to buy either one.
One side is outside the sample. 1MD Complete Probiotics Platinum is not in the captured Amazon ranking the pilot was drawn from — it is scored on the identical six criteria under the v1.15 off-sample ruling, from a separate file, and is counted in no ranking or mean on this site. Where they agree

Both labels were scored the same way on the remaining 6.

0 / 0
1. Exact amount for every active ingredient

Physician's CHOICE Probiotics 60 Billion CFU: Probiotic Blend 220 mg and Prebiotic Blend 150 mg give blend totals only, no per-ingredient amounts.

1MD Complete Probiotics Platinum: The 11 organisms sit inside a single 308 mg blend total with no per-organism amount or CFU split. NutraFlora FOS at 50 mg does carry an exact amount.

1 / 1
2. Standardization percentage stated

Physician's CHOICE Probiotics 60 Billion CFU: No standardization-dependent ingredient in the formula.

1MD Complete Probiotics Platinum: By default. Nothing in the formula is standardization-dependent: the probiotics are declared by CFU and weight, and FOS is declared as the compound itself (fructo-oligosaccharides) rather than as an extract weight, the same distinction applied to Culturelle Pro Strength's inulin and not available to BIOMA's XOS.

0 / 0
3. Chemical form or strain named

Physician's CHOICE Probiotics 60 Billion CFU: Ten organisms named to species only (Lacticaseibacillus casei, Lactobacillus acidophilus, ... Lactobacillus bulgaricus), no strain designations, per methodology v1.6.

1MD Complete Probiotics Platinum: Ten of the eleven organisms are named to species only; only Bacillus coagulans MTCC 5856 carries a strain designation, and one undesignated organism is enough to score 0 under v1.6.

0 / 0
4. Dose within the trial range

Physician's CHOICE Probiotics 60 Billion CFU: Prebiotic Blend totals 150 mg including inulin from Jerusalem artichoke and chicory, about eighty times below the 12 g/d inulin reference range even if the blend were entirely inulin; probiotics excluded per methodology.

1MD Complete Probiotics Platinum: The probiotics cannot be compared to any trial dose, since every organism sits inside a blend total (attribution rule) and the panel gives no through-expiry guarantee (the brand's own product page FAQ states guaranteed potency through the expiration date, but that is not on the label read here). That leaves FOS, declared at 50 mg against a reference range of 5 g/day (clinical-doses.csv, Paineau 2008, PMID 17697398), so the disclosed dose is one hundredth of the only positive-outcome trial dose and falls outside it.

0 / 0
5. Certificate of analysis is public

Physician's CHOICE Probiotics 60 Billion CFU: No publicly accessible third-party certificate of analysis located 2026-09-07 (product page, brand site, site-scoped search, retail listing images); recorded as a negative finding, not a claim that none exists.

1MD Complete Probiotics Platinum: No publicly accessible third-party certificate of analysis located 2026-11-19 (Amazon listing and its images, 1md.org product page including its FAQ markup, 1md.org site search for 'certificate of analysis', which returned this product page but no certificate on it, site-scoped and open web search). Listing image 3 is a 'Testing & Analysis' graphic stating each batch is tested by a third-party lab and showing pass ticks for microbial and heavy metal tests, with no laboratory name, lot number, results or document: a testing claim rather than a certificate, excluded on the same basis as Ritual's Certificate of Traceability. Recorded as a negative finding, not a claim that none exists.

1 / 1
6. Per-serving amounts are complete

Physician's CHOICE Probiotics 60 Billion CFU: Serving size 1 capsule and 30 servings per container both stated.

1MD Complete Probiotics Platinum: Serving size 1 capsule and servings per container 30 are both printed on the panel.
