Where they differ

2. Standardization percentage stated

NewRhythm Probiotics 50 Billion CFU: This is a judgment call worth stating. No ingredient identified on the panel is standardization-dependent: the actives are live organisms, and the prebiotic blend names nothing that requires a marker percentage. The criterion says in terms that a product scores 1 by default when nothing in the formula requires a stated percentage. The uncomfortable consequence is that a blend disclosing no ingredients cannot be shown to need a percentage, so non-disclosure is not penalised here. That non-disclosure is already captured, heavily, by criterion 1, and scoring it twice would double-count one failure. Flagged for review.

1MD BiomeMD: XOS is sold at graded purities and its reference range is expressed as XOS, so a percentage is potency-determining under the functional test; here neither the percentage nor even an XOS weight is disclosed, since it sits inside a blend total. Scored consistently with BIOMA, Lemme Debloat and Garden of Life Men.

NewRhythm Probiotics 50 Billion CFU: Two blends. Probiotic Blend gives only an aggregate 50 Billion CFU across 20 organisms, and Premium Prebiotic Fiber Blend gives only a 150 mg total with no ingredients named at all.

1MD BiomeMD: Two blends. The BiomeMD Probiotic Blend gives a 121 mg total for 13 organisms with no per-organism amount, and the Advanced Prebiotic Blend gives a 380 mg total for three fibers with no per-fiber amount. UABla-12 and DDS-1 are the only actives with their own amounts.

3. Chemical form or strain named

NewRhythm Probiotics 50 Billion CFU: All 20 organisms are named to genus and species only, most of them abbreviated (L. acidophilus, B. lactis, Pediococcus pentosaceus and so on), with no strain designations anywhere. The listing title and the bottle front both advertise '20 Strains'. Third product in the sample to market strain counts while naming no strains.

1MD BiomeMD: The 13 organisms in the BiomeMD Probiotic Blend are named to genus and species only (Lactobacillus plantarum, Bacillus coagulans and so on). UABla-12 and DDS-1 carry strain designations, but one undesignated organism is enough to score 0 under v1.6.

0 / 0
4. Dose within the trial range

NewRhythm Probiotics 50 Billion CFU: The probiotics are an aggregate with no per-organism split and no strain designations, so nothing can be matched to a trial under the attribution rule. On v1.13, the footnote reads 'Contains a minimum of 50 billion live bacteria when manufactured, and provides viable levels until at least the best by date'. The at-manufacture count is a number; the through-expiry assurance is the phrase 'viable levels', which is not a quantity. No delivered dose is established. The contrast with NOW Probiotic-10, whose label commits to a specific figure through the Best By date, is the distinction v1.13 was written to draw. The prebiotic blend discloses no composition.

1MD BiomeMD: On the fibers alone. The Advanced Prebiotic Blend is 380 mg in total, so even if all of it were XOS it would sit below the 1.4 g/day low end of the XOS range, and even if all of it were FOS it would be under a tenth of the 5 g/day FOS range: the same upper-bound test applied to Physician's CHOICE's 150 mg prebiotic blend. The probiotics give no point back. The 13 blended organisms cannot be compared to any trial dose under the attribution rule. UABla-12 and DDS-1 are declared both in milligrams and in CFU. The milligram figures cannot be used under v1.12: the human trial located for these strains at a digestive outcome, Martoni 2020 (PMID 32019158, randomised double-blind placebo-controlled, 330 adults with IBS, conducted with the strain supplier UAS Laboratories, so the weaker basis under the independence rule), doses in CFU, not milligrams of preparation: 1 x 10^10 CFU/day per strain (Martoni 2020 full text, mdpi.com), the same figure as the label's unqualified 10 billion CFU, so the 0 rests on the v1.13 rule that an unqualified count does not establish a delivered dose. Its introduction cites earlier trials of the same strains in functional constipation and lactose intolerance; those were not reviewed for this row, which is recorded as a limit of the search (PubMed 'Lactobacillus acidophilus DDS-1 irritable bowel randomized', 2026-11-19, 2 records). The CFU figures carry no through-expiry guarantee anywhere on the panel, so under v1.13 the delivered dose cannot be established and criterion 4 scores 0 on both ingredients.

5. Certificate of analysis is public

NewRhythm Probiotics 50 Billion CFU: No publicly accessible third-party certificate of analysis located 2026-09-27 (listing images, brand site, site-scoped and open web search). The bottle front prints '3RD PARTY TESTED' and listing image 6, headed 'We take test seriously', shows specification tables for microbiological and heavy metal contamination with generic passed marks, carrying no laboratory name, no batch or lot number, no numerical results and no date. That is a testing claim without a document behind it, which the methodology excludes explicitly. Recorded as a negative finding, not a claim that none exists.

1MD BiomeMD: No publicly accessible third-party certificate of analysis located 2026-11-19 (Amazon listing and its images, 1md.org/products/biomemd, 1md.org site search, site-scoped and open web search). Listing image 3 is a 'Testing & Analysis' graphic claiming third-party batch testing with pass ticks and no laboratory, lot or document, excluded as a testing claim; the bottle it shows is labelled BiomeMD for Women, a different product with a different panel. Recorded as a negative finding, not a claim that none exists.
