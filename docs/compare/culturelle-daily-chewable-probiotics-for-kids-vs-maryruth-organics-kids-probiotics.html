3. Chemical form or strain named

Culturelle Daily Chewable Probiotics for Kids: LGG is a strain designation, and it is the example the methodology itself uses for criterion 3. C4 0, and on dose rather than on any unit or disclosure technicality. The label delivers 5x10^9 CFU per day. Vanderhoof 1999 (PMID 10547243), the pediatric LGG trial, dosed 1x10^10 to 2x10^10 CFU/day in children aged 6 months to 10 years and found a significant reduction in antibiotic-associated diarrhoea. This product provides one quarter to one half of the trial dose. The clinical-doses.csv row for LGG was carrying a 2026-09-07 flag reading 'NOT YET VERIFIED against PubMed... verify before relying on it'; it was verified and replaced with a real range before this score was assigned.

MaryRuth Organics Kids Probiotics: DE111 is a strain designation, not the species alone.

0 / 0
4. Dose within the trial range

Culturelle Daily Chewable Probiotics for Kids: No reasoning recorded.

MaryRuth Organics Kids Probiotics: No positive-outcome human trial for DE111 at a gastrointestinal outcome was identified on 2026-09-27, and no paediatric trial of the strain was identified at any dose, which is the population this product is sold for. The two DE111 trials that measured gut endpoints both reported no effect (Freedman 2021 PMID 33671071; Townsend 2018 PMID 30049931), and under the positive-outcome-trials-only rule a null trial does not set a range. The reference table row records the full search per the negative-findings rule. Note the label itself is not the problem here: it discloses the dose in both mg and CFU and names the strain, so a buyer could check it if the evidence existed to check it against. The failure is in the evidence base, and the objection recorded under v1.11 applies with full force to this row.

0 / 0
5. Certificate of analysis is public

Culturelle Daily Chewable Probiotics for Kids: No publicly accessible third-party certificate of analysis located 2026-09-27 (listing images, culturelle.com, site-scoped and open web search), consistent with the same finding for Culturelle Pro Strength on 2026-09-26. Recorded as a negative finding, not a claim that none exists.

MaryRuth Organics Kids Probiotics: No publicly accessible third-party certificate of analysis located 2026-09-27 (listing images, maryruthorganics.com including the quality page, site-scoped and open web search). The listing and site display Clean Label Project Purity Award and B Corp certification marks, which are certifications rather than product certificates of analysis and are excluded on the same basis as GMP marks. Recorded as a negative finding, not a claim that none exists.
