Where they differ

The pair splits on 2 of the six criteria. The reasoning below is the same text that appears on each product page.

0 / 1
1. Exact amount for every active ingredient

Lemme Debloat Digestive & Gut Health Gummies: 'Digestive Probiotic Blend 44 mg' gives a blend total for two organisms with no per-organism amount, and notably no CFU count anywhere on the panel at all, which is unusual even among the blends in this sample. Xylooligosaccharides 80 mg does carry an exact amount.

Digestive Advantage Probiotic Gummies: Both actives carry exact amounts, Vitamin D (as cholecalciferol) 8 mcg and BC30 Bacillus coagulans GBI-30, 6086 at 4.67 mg (1 Billion CFUs). No proprietary blend on the panel.

0 / 1
2. Standardization percentage stated

Lemme Debloat Digestive & Gut Health Gummies: XOS is declared as 80 mg with no purity percentage. This is the same case as BIOMA, where commercial XOS is sold at graded purities from roughly 35 to 95 percent and the reference range is expressed as XOS rather than as extract weight. Scored consistently with that row.

Digestive Advantage Probiotic Gummies: No standardization-dependent ingredient in the formula.

Where they agree

Both labels were scored the same way on the remaining 4.

1 / 1
3. Chemical form or strain named

Lemme Debloat Digestive & Gut Health Gummies: Both organisms carry strain designations, Bacillus coagulans MTCC 5856 (LactoSpore) and Bacillus subtilis DE111. The listing's claim of 'two clinically studied strains' is, unlike several other products in this sample, actually supported by the panel.

Digestive Advantage Probiotic Gummies: Both forms are named. Vitamin D is specified as cholecalciferol rather than 'vitamin D', and the probiotic carries the full depositary strain designation GBI-30, 6086.

0 / 0
4. Dose within the trial range

Lemme Debloat Digestive & Gut Health Gummies: XOS at 80 mg falls far below the 1.4 to 8 g/day reference range, reaching under 6 percent of the low end even if the 80 mg were 100 percent XOS, which the label does not establish. The probiotics sit inside a 44 mg blend total with no CFU declared, so neither strain can be compared to any trial dose under the attribution rule, and under v1.13 no delivered potency is established either.

Digestive Advantage Probiotic Gummies: Under methodology v1.13. The CFU figure is marked with a double-dagger footnote that is never defined anywhere on the Supplement Facts panel: the panel defines *, ** and the single dagger, and the double dagger appears only against '1 Billion CFUs'. No at-manufacture qualifier and no through-expiry guarantee is therefore established, and the delivered dose cannot be determined. Independently, the only trial identified for this strain at this outcome (Hun 2009, PMID 19332970) states no CFU dose in its abstract, so no reference range exists to compare against even if the delivered dose were established. Vitamin D at 8 mcg is a nutrient at roughly 40 percent of the adult Daily Value, not a dose aimed at the bloating and abdominal discomfort outcome this product claims.

0 / 0
5. Certificate of analysis is public

Lemme Debloat Digestive & Gut Health Gummies: No publicly accessible third-party certificate of analysis located 2026-09-27 (listing images, brand site, site-scoped and open web search). Listing image 5 displays a Clean Label Project Purity Award, which is a certification mark rather than a product certificate of analysis and is excluded on the same basis the methodology excludes GMP and facility certificates. Recorded as a negative finding, not a claim that none exists.

Digestive Advantage Probiotic Gummies: No publicly accessible third-party certificate of analysis located 2026-09-27 (listing images, the schiffvitamins.com product page, site-scoped and open web search). The page carries the claim that the 'clinically tested BC30 probiotic' survives stomach acid '100x better versus yogurts', with no document behind it, which the methodology excludes explicitly. Recorded as a negative finding, not a claim that none exists.
