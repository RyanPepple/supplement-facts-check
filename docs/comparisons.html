Comparisons

15 head-to-head pages, each one putting two scored labels side by side on the same six criteria. Every figure on them is read from the same two rows as the two product pages, so a comparison cannot disagree with the pages it compares.

What a comparison does not say. A higher score means the label discloses more, not that the product is better or that it works. Nothing here was lab-tested, and no product on this site is recommended over another. Only products scored in the pilot can be compared, so a pair a shopper might want is missing whenever one of the two is not yet scored.
