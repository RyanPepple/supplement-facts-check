Headline finding · criterion 3 of 6

12 of 18 best-selling gut supplements name every chemical form or probiotic strain

12 of the 18 best-selling gut-health supplements audited by Supplement Facts Check name the chemical form or probiotic strain of every active ingredient. The other 6 do not: at least one organism or mineral is named without its strain or form.

The sample. 18 products scored so far from the top 30 of Amazon’s Best Sellers in Probiotic Nutritional Supplements, captured 09/26/2026. Every inclusion and exclusion decision is listed on the results page. Read these as pilot results, not a ranking.

Criterion 3: Chemical form or strain named

“Magnesium glycinate,” not “magnesium.” For probiotics, every organism carries a strain designation. It scores 1 or 0, with no weighting and no partial credit, under methodology v1.13, locked 2026-09-27. Read the full methodology →
