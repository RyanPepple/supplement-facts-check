Only 7 of 18 best-selling gut supplements disclose an exact amount for every active ingredient

Only 7 of the 18 best-selling gut-health supplements audited by Supplement Facts Check disclose an exact amount for every active ingredient. The other 11 do not: the panel hides at least one active ingredient inside a proprietary blend.

The sample. 18 products scored so far from the top 30 of Amazon’s Best Sellers in Probiotic Nutritional Supplements, captured 09/26/2026. Every inclusion and exclusion decision is listed on the results page. Read these as pilot results, not a ranking.

Criterion 1: Exact amount for every active ingredient

No proprietary blend appears anywhere on the Supplement Facts panel. It scores 1 or 0, with no weighting and no partial credit, under methodology v1.14, locked 2026-10-26. Read the full methodology →
