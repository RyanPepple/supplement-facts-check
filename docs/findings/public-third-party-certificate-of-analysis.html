Headline finding · criterion 5 of 6

None of 18 best-selling gut supplements publish a publicly accessible third-party certificate of analysis

None of the 18 best-selling gut-health supplements audited by Supplement Facts Check publish a third-party certificate of analysis reachable with no email gate and no account. For all 18, a documented search found no publicly accessible third-party certificate.

The sample. 18 products scored so far from the top 30 of Amazon’s Best Sellers in Probiotic Nutritional Supplements, captured 09/26/2026. Every inclusion and exclusion decision is listed on the results page. Read these as pilot results, not a ranking.
What a 0 means here. A 0 records that a documented search — product page, brand site, site-scoped search and the retail listing — did not find a publicly accessible third-party certificate on the date shown. That is a negative finding about public accessibility, not a claim that no certificate exists or that a product is contaminated.

Criterion 5: Certificate of analysis is public

A third-party certificate is reachable with no email gate and no account. It scores 1 or 0, with no weighting and no partial credit, under methodology v1.14, locked 2026-10-26. Read the full methodology →
