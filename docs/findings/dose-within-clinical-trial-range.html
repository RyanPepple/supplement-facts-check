Just one of 18 best-selling gut supplements discloses a dose inside the clinical trial range

Just one of the 18 best-selling gut-health supplements audited by Supplement Facts Check discloses a dose inside the range human trials used for the outcome claimed. The other 17 do not: the disclosed dose falls outside the trial range, or no dose is disclosed to check.

The sample. 18 products scored so far from the top 30 of Amazon’s Best Sellers in Probiotic Nutritional Supplements, captured 09/26/2026. Every inclusion and exclusion decision is listed on the results page. Read these as pilot results, not a ranking.

Criterion 4: Dose within the trial range

The disclosed dose falls inside the range human trials used for the outcome the product claims. It scores 1 or 0, with no weighting and no partial credit, under methodology v1.14, locked 2026-10-26. Read the full methodology →

NOW Supplements Probiotic-10
