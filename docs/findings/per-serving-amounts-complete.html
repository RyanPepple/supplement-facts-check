12 of 18 best-selling gut supplements state both serving size and servings per container

12 of the 18 best-selling gut-health supplements audited by Supplement Facts Check state both the serving size and the servings per container. The other 6 do not: serving size or servings per container is absent from the panel.

The sample. 18 products scored so far from the top 30 of Amazon’s Best Sellers in Probiotic Nutritional Supplements, captured 09/26/2026. Every inclusion and exclusion decision is listed on the results page. Read these as pilot results, not a ranking.

Criterion 6: Per-serving amounts are complete

Serving size and servings per container are both on the panel. It scores 1 or 0, with no weighting and no partial credit, under methodology v1.14, locked 2026-10-26. Read the full methodology →
