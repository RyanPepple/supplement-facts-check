Headline finding · criterion 2 of 6

12 of 18 best-selling gut supplements state the standardization percentage

12 of the 18 best-selling gut-health supplements audited by Supplement Facts Check state the standardization percentage wherever potency depends on one. The other 6 do not: an ingredient whose potency depends on a percentage carries no percentage.

The sample. 18 products scored so far from the top 30 of Amazon’s Best Sellers in Probiotic Nutritional Supplements, captured 09/26/2026. Every inclusion and exclusion decision is listed on the results page. Read these as pilot results, not a ranking.

Criterion 2: Standardization percentage stated

Wherever potency depends on a percentage, such as % withanolides, the label states it. It scores 1 or 0, with no weighting and no partial credit, under methodology v1.13, locked 2026-09-27. Read the full methodology →
